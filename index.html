Quadrature-Based Moment Methods · for OpenFOAM

Polydisperse and reacting flows, in moments

OpenQBMM is a suite of solvers to simulate turbulent reacting flows and polydisperse multiphase flows using Quadrature-Based Moment Methods (QBMM) based on OpenFOAM®.

Get started Source on GitHub

Capabilities

From the size evolution of non-inertial particles to gas-particle flows with highly inertial particles.

Univariate PBE

QMOM and GQMOM

The quadrature method of moments and the generalized quadrature method of moments for the solution of generalized population balance equations.

Multivariate

CQMOM and HyQMOM

The conditional quadrature method of moments and its hyperbolic version, for multivariate problems.

Kinetic equations

Velocity distribution transport

Dispersed flows with non-equilibrium velocity distributions — crossing jets of particles or droplets, with size evolving in space and time.

Numerics

Realizable schemes

Second-order realizable moment advection schemes and a realizable ODE solver, so transported moment sets stay physical.

About the figure up top

No numbers were harmed in its making: they are all real. The 46 particles are a random sample of a log-normal distribution of sizes (σ = 0.3), and the histogram counts them, under the distribution they were drawn from. The four nodes are its Gauss quadrature, found from its first eight moments by the algorithm of Golub and Welsch, and they give those eight moments back exactly. The fourth node sits far out in the tail with a weight of 0.002, which is what a skewed distribution does to a quadrature. A whole distribution, in a few moments.
