What the award funded

The project ran for three years from October 1st, 2014, and covered the following work.

  • 01 The development of computational models for turbulent multiphase flows in the framework of quadrature-based moment methods.
  • 02 The implementation of a suite of three solvers into OpenFOAM, addressing the population balance equation for particles with negligible inertia, gas-liquid flows where bubble inertia is small but not zero, and gas-particle flows with highly inertial particles.
  • 03 The creation of documentation, verification and validation cases, and tutorials to favor the adoption of the software and external contributions to it.
  • 04 The distribution of the developed software under the GNU GPL 3 license, and of its documentation under the GNU FDL, in order to disseminate the results of the research and gather feedback.
