Applications

What OpenQBMM simulates

Example applications of OpenQBMM. Click on the case name to reach the directory with the case sources; the Results link takes you to the case on the validation page.

  • Particle size distributions
  • Turbulent mixing and reacting flows
  • Granular and gas-particle flows
  • Sprays
  • High-speed compressible flows

pbeFoam · pbeTransportFoam

Particle size distributions

Aggregation, breakage, growth and condensation of populations of particles, droplets or bubbles, solved for the moments of their size distribution with QMOM, GQMOM and their variants, in a single cell or transported in a flow.

  • Aggregation and breakage
  • GQMOM against rigorous solutions results
  • Condensation, after Lage results
  • Diffusion-controlled growth, after McGraw results
  • Convergence with the number of moments results
  • A Taylor–Couette reactor

mixingFoam · mixingReactingFoam · pbeTransportFoam

Turbulent mixing and reacting flows

Streams that mix and react in turbulent flow, the composition and the enthalpy carried by the moments of the mixture fraction, with IEM and Fokker–Planck mixing models, and a population balance in each environment of the mixing.

  • Mixing by interaction and exchange with the mean (IEM)
  • Fokker–Planck mixing
  • Mixing with a reaction, GQMOM results
  • Two streams and a fast reaction results
  • A methane plug-flow reactor results
  • Mixing and a population balance results

vdfTransportFoam · diluteVdfTransportFoam · denseAGFoam

Granular and gas-particle flows

Particles whose velocities are not those of the fluid: crossing jets, granular gases cooling by inelastic collisions, risers and fluidized beds, solved for the moments of the velocity distribution, or with an anisotropic Gaussian closure.

  • Crossing particle jets
  • Cooling of a bidisperse granular gas results
  • Wall-bounded granular cooling, CHyQMOM
  • A riser
  • Particles around a rotating cylinder
  • A fluidized bed

oneWayCoupledVdfTransportFoam

Sprays

Droplets of many sizes and velocities carried by a gas, their sizes and velocities described jointly by moments, with coalescence.

  • One-dimensional sprays, with and without coalescence results
  • Particles in Taylor–Green vortices
  • Particles of many sizes in Taylor–Green vortices

compressiblePbeTransportFoam

High-speed compressible flows

A population balance in high-speed compressible flow, through shocks and expansions.

  • A forward-facing step
  • A 23-degree wedge
